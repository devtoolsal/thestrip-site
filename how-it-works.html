HOW IT WORKS

From first message
to last photo.

A simple process, with room for the details that make your event yours.

01

Enquire.

Send us the date, the venue and a little about the occasion. We’ll check availability and come back with questions.

02

We tailor.

We shape the hours, the prints and the look around your event, then send a clear quote and booking details.

03

We arrive.

We deliver, set up and stay with the booth. You don’t have to manage a thing.

On the night.

The Strip arrives with its attendant, settles into the room, and stays ready. Guests step in, close the curtain, and take as many moments as they like.

Prints come out on the night. A digital gallery follows, so the photographs don’t stay behind on a table.

Space and power.

The booth is compact, but every venue is different. We’ll confirm the footprint, access and a standard power point with you and your venue before the day.

Before the day.

Once the booking is confirmed, we’ll agree the arrival time, the setup window and the look of the prints. If you’re planning something unexpected, tell us early — that’s usually the fun part.

After the night.

You keep the prints. We’ll send the digital gallery so the photographs can travel further than the venue.
