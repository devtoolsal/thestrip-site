TERMS

The practical bits.

Booking, payment and the things worth agreeing before the night.

Last updated: 18 November 2026. These are the general terms for The Strip. The specific details of your booking — date, hours, venue, quote and cancellation window — are set out in your booking agreement.

Quotes and bookings

An enquiry is not a booking. A booking is confirmed once the details, quote and booking terms are agreed and any required deposit is received. Dates are held only once confirmed.

Payment

Payment terms, including any deposit and the balance due date, are set out in your quote. Work outside the agreed scope may be quoted separately.

Cancellation and changes

The cancellation and rescheduling terms for your event are set out in your booking agreement. Tell us as early as possible if anything changes; we will always try to find a workable option.

Venue, space and power

You are responsible for confirming that the venue allows the booth, that the space and access are suitable, and that a standard power point is available. We will confirm the footprint and requirements with you before the event.

Setup and operation

We arrive within the agreed setup window and operate the booth for the agreed hours. Delays caused by the venue, access or power may affect the operating time.

Photographs and prints

We will make reasonable efforts to deliver the prints and digital gallery described in your booking. Print quantities and gallery details are confirmed in your quote.

Damage and liability

Please treat the booth and its props with care. You may be responsible for loss or damage caused by you or your guests. Our liability is limited to the amount paid for the affected booking, to the extent permitted by law.

Imagery

We may ask permission to share selected photographs from your event. We will not use identifiable images for marketing without consent.

Contact

Questions about these terms: hello@thestripphotobooth.co.za.
