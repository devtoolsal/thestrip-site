01 / THE EXPERIENCE

Step in.
Let yourself out.

An enclosed photobooth for the moments that happen when the formalities are over.

A little space to be yourself.

The Strip is built around a simple idea: the best photographs happen when people forget they’re being photographed. Close the curtain, and the room disappears. The poses loosen. The real faces come out.

It’s an enclosed booth — intimate, a little theatrical, and entirely yours for the moment. Step in with your favourite people, press the button, and let the strip tell the story.

Made to feel like part of the room.

Warm wood, soft light and a curtain that does its job. The Strip is designed to sit beautifully in a venue, not shout over it — and to feel like a small event of its own.

Keepsakes, not just photographs.

You leave with a printed strip in your hand and a digital copy to keep, share and look back on. Something tangible from a night that went by too quickly.
