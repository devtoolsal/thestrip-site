FAQ

Good questions.

The things people usually ask before they step behind the curtain.

What is The Strip?

The Strip is an enclosed photobooth. You step behind a curtain, take your photographs in private, and leave with a printed strip and a digital copy. It’s built for weddings, parties, launches and any night that deserves a record.

Is it really private?

Yes. The booth is enclosed and the curtain closes behind you. That bit of privacy is exactly what makes the photographs feel different.

How much space do you need?

The booth is compact, but the right spot depends on the venue and the flow of the room. Tell us about the space and we’ll confirm the footprint and access with you.

What about power?

A standard power point is all we need. We’ll confirm the details with you and the venue before the event.

How many people fit?

Enough for the moment — couples, friends, the whole wedding party. Step in, squeeze in, see what happens.

Do we get prints and digital copies?

Yes. Guests take printed strips home on the night, and a digital gallery follows so the photographs can be shared and kept.

How long does setup take?

We arrive ahead of the event and set up before guests need the booth. The exact window is agreed when we confirm the booking.

Do you travel?

Tell us where the event is. Travel is quoted as part of your proposal, so there are no surprises.

Can we customise the prints?

Yes. Print design, a branded backdrop and other details can be tailored to your event or brand.

Do you have an attendant?

Yes. The booth comes with an attendant who sets up, stays with it and makes sure the night runs smoothly.

How do we book?

Send an enquiry with your date and venue. We’ll confirm availability, shape the details and send a tailored quote. The booking is confirmed once the details and terms are agreed.

What is the cancellation policy?

The booking terms, including cancellation, are set out clearly in your quote and booking agreement before you confirm.

Still wondering about something? Ask us directly.
