PRIVACY

Privacy, plainly.

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to ask us to stop.

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Who we are

The Strip is a photobooth service operating in South Africa. For privacy questions, contact hello@thestripphotobooth.co.za.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect your name, email address, phone number if you provide it, event date, occasion, venue or town, and anything you choose to tell us in your message.

If you use the booth, we process the photographs taken in it for the purpose of printing them and providing the digital gallery. Photographs may contain personal information and images of people.

Why we collect it

We use enquiry details to respond to you, prepare a quote and manage a booking. We use event photographs to provide the service you booked. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for as long as needed to respond and manage the relationship, and for reasonable business records afterwards. Photographs and galleries are kept for the period agreed for your event. Ask us if you want a specific retention period.

Who we share it with

We use service providers for hosting, email, payments and photo delivery where needed to run the service. We only share what is necessary for those services, and we do not sell data.

Your rights

Under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to processing. Email hello@thestripphotobooth.co.za and we will respond.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will update this page and ask for consent where required.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No system is completely secure; please avoid sending sensitive information through the enquiry form.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service grows. The date at the top shows the latest version.
